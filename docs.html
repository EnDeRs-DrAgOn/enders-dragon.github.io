16x16 Fairy Chess

16x16 Fairy Chess Reference Guide

1. Common Tier (White Outlines)

  • P — Standard Engine Pawn Your main infantry line. It moves forward up to 3 squares on its first move, up to 2 squares on its second move, and 1 square forward per turn thereafter. Captures like a base game pawn. Can be en passanted on a 3-square move.
  • BP — Berolina Pawn An inverted pawn. It moves diagonally forward up to 3 squares on its first move, up to 2 on its second, and 1 thereafter. It captures by stepping exactly 1 square straight forward.
  • Af — Alfil A classic fairy chess piece that leaps exactly 2 squares diagonally, jumping over any intermediate piece.

2. Rare Tier (Green Outlines)

  • AP — Arrow Pawn A ranged pawn variant. It steps up to 3 squares forward initially and 2 thereafter. Instead of moving diagonally to capture, it shoots an enemy piece sitting 2 squares away diagonally, then moves 1 square diagonally forward to pick up the arrow.
  • Ba — Baronet A localized power piece. It can leap like a Knight or slide exactly 1 square in any of the 8 grid directions.
  • Al — Alibaba A compound leaper that can jump exactly 2 squares diagonally (Alfil leap) or exactly 2 squares orthogonally (Dabbaba leap).

3. Epic Tier (Blue Outlines)

  • Ch — Chancellor Combines the power of a Rook and a Knight.
  • Ab — Archbishop Combines the power of a Bishop and a Knight.
  • Ca — Castle Slides like a Rook, but can also slide exactly 1 square diagonally.
  • AfR — Alfilrider The promoted version of the Alfil. It can slide any distance along diagonal paths, but only land on every second square.

4. Legendary Tier (Purple Outlines)

  • E — Elephant Moves like a Rook, but can jump over any non-pawn pieces blocking its line of sight.
  • Ex — Executioner Slides diagonally like a Bishop. When it lands adjacent to an enemy Amazon, it sacrifices itself to eliminate the Amazon and unlock the Amazonian for that player.
  • ZN — Zigzag Nightrider A long-range leaper that chains Knight moves together in an alternating zigzag path.
  • AlR — Alibabarider The promoted version of the Alibaba. It can travel indefinitely along orthogonal or diagonal tracks, skipping every other square.

5. Divine Highest Tier (Gold Outlines)

  • M — Mortar A stationary artillery piece with a diamond-shaped shell range. It moves and captures normally like a King, one square in any direction. Instead of moving, however, it can fire a shell at an enemy piece anywhere within a Manhattan distance of 4. Shells may pass over friendly pieces, but cannot pass through enemy pieces. Firing a shell does not move the Mortar.
  • K — King The crown piece. Moves 1 square in any direction. Its preservation determines the win or loss state of the match.
  • A — Amazon A powerful compound piece combining the movement profiles of the Queen and Knight, with its special jumping mechanics.
  • R — Rose Moves along a circular path of Knight leaps, allowing multiple consecutive 2×1 curves in a single turn when unobstructed.
  • Am — Amazonian A locked endgame piece unlocked through an Executioner sacrifice. It combines Queen, Knight, and Rose movement.
  • J — Jester A mimic piece with no fixed movement profile. It copies the movement rules of the piece moved by the opponent on the previous turn. If no piece has moved yet, it falls back to King movement.

6. Legacy Class Tier (Rose Brown Outlines)

  • 0Q — Original Queen Moves any number of squares in any direction.
  • 0R — Original Rook Moves any number of squares orthogonally.
  • 0B — Original Bishop Moves any number of squares diagonally.
  • 0N — Original Knight Leaps in the standard 2×1 Knight pattern and can jump over other pieces.
  • 0P — Original Pawn Follows standard chess pawn movement, including its initial two-square move, diagonal captures, and en passant.
